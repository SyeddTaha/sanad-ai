# SANAD

SANAD supports evidence-first verification and disputes concerning synthetic relief payment notices.

## Language

**Signed Notice**:
An issuer's digitally signed statement about a synthetic relief payment notice; a valid signature establishes issuer origin and content integrity, not payment completion or recipient eligibility.
_Avoid_: Verified payment, payment proof

**Evidence Passport**:
A reviewable record of independent findings about signature validity, notice expiry and revocation, recipient matching, payment status, and eligibility, including their sources, timestamps, and applicable rule versions. Unknown findings remain unknown, and earlier findings remain part of its history when evidence changes.
_Avoid_: Overall verification verdict, safe/scam verdict

**Case**:
A reviewable dispute or request with a workflow status independent of its evidence findings. Resolution or closure does not change the underlying findings or their history.
_Avoid_: Verification result, payment approval
